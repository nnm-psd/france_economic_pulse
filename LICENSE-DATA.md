The raw data in `data/raw/` and `data/snapshots/` is redistributed unchanged under each provider's terms, which
also require attribution. When you reuse it, credit the original source:

| Source | Data | Licence |
|---|---|---|
| RTE, via ODRE | éCO2mix electricity consumption | [Licence Ouverte / Etalab 2.0](https://www.etalab.gouv.fr/licence-ouverte-open-licence/) |
| INSEE | Industrial production index, business climate | [Licence Ouverte / Etalab 2.0](https://www.etalab.gouv.fr/licence-ouverte-open-licence/) |
| DILA | BODACC insolvency notices | [Licence Ouverte / Etalab 2.0](https://www.etalab.gouv.fr/licence-ouverte-open-licence/) |

Etalab 2.0 is designed to be compatible with CC BY, which is why CC BY 4.0 was chosen for this project's own data.
